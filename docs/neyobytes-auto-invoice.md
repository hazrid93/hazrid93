# Neyobytes Auto Invoice

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This is an ongoing, private project. Most details are secret and confidential, including product, implementation, and architecture information.** They are intentionally not published. This page contains only a limited public overview.

**[🌐 Visit Neyobytes Auto Invoice](https://autoinvoice.neyobytes.com/)**

Neyobytes Auto Invoice is an AI-assisted e-Invoice product being developed for Malaysian micro-businesses and SMEs. It aims to make invoice capture, review, record keeping, and e-Invoice preparation simpler for everyday business users.

## Public Overview

- Designed around Malaysian e-Invoice workflows.
- Helps users capture and review invoice information.
- Focuses on a simple, mobile-friendly user experience.
- Built with TypeScript and modern web/mobile technologies.
- Currently under active development.
